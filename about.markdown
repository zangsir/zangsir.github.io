I am currently a principal machine learning research engineer at [Bose](https://bose.com/) Research (2017-present). Previously I was a [researcher](https://www.upf.edu/web/mtg/about/former-members) at the [Music Technology Group (MTG)][mtg], Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona, Spain. I received my Ph.D from Georgetown University (USA) with a focus on computational linguistics and natural language processing. 

My research interest is machine learning for NLP and audio. Previously I worked on speech prosody, coreference resolution, audio time-series and text data mining, with applications in Music Information Retrieval (MIR) and computational musicology. I also contributed to NLP and Digital Humanities open source software such as [ANNIS](https://corpus-tools.org/annis/) and [GitDOX](https://gucorpling.org/gitdox/).  Currently I am focusing on deep learning for audio applications, including audio understanding, source separation, and generative audio.
