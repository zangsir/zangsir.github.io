I am a machine learning researcher and engineering lead at [Bose](https://bose.com/) Research since 2017. Previously I was a [researcher](https://www.upf.edu/web/mtg/about/former-members) at the [Music Technology Group (MTG)][mtg], Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona, Spain. I received my Ph.D from Georgetown University (USA) with a focus on computational linguistics and natural language processing. 

My research interest is deep learning for NLP and audio. Previously I worked on CompMusic Project at MTG, as well as speech prosody, NLP, Music Information Retrieval (MIR) and computational musicology. Currently I am focusing on deep learning for audio applications, including audio understanding, source separation, and generative audio. 

[mtg]: https://mtg.upf.edu
