I received my Ph.D and M.S. from Georgetown University (USA) with a focus on [computational linguistics](https://gucl.georgetown.edu/) and natural language processing. Before that I received my M.A from the Department of Music, University of Pittsburgh and my B.S. from the College of Environmetal Science and Engineering, Peking University. My research interest is machine learning for NLP and audio.
