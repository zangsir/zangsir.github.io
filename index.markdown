I am currently a principal machine learning researcher at [Bose](https://bose.com/) Research and a Professor of the Practice at [Tufts University](https://as.tufts.edu/dataanalytics/people/faculty/shuo-zhang). Previously I was a [researcher](https://www.upf.edu/web/mtg/about/former-members) at the [CompMusic Project](https://compmusic.upf.edu/), [Music Technology Group (MTG)](https://mtg.upf.edu), Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona, Spain, under Prof. [Xavier Serra](https://www.upf.edu/web/xavier-serra). I received my Ph.D and M.S. from Georgetown University (USA) with a focus on [computational linguistics](https://gucl.georgetown.edu/) and natural language processing under Prof. [Amir Zeldes](https://gufaculty360.georgetown.edu/s/contact/00336000014TM4fAAG/amir-zeldes). Before that I received my M.A from the Department of Music, University of Pittsburgh under Prof. [Bell Yung](http://www.bellyung.com/), and my B.S. from the College of Environmetal Science and Engineering, Peking University. In 2016, I co-taught a tutorial of NLP and MIR at ISMIR 2016 at Columbia University. Since 2021, I have been active on the organizing committee of [DCASE Workshop](https://dcase.community/). My research interest is machine learning for NLP and audio, and broadly in areas such as computational musicology, speech prosody, coreference resolution, music information retrieval, audio-text models, source separation, generative audio, audio understanding, trustworthy AI, with applications in audio products.
